# hydrogen-sabre-engine
im working on a hydrogen powered hybrid engine similar to the sabre engine that is a mix between a rocket and a jet. 
this thing is the brain child of my love for rockets and jet engines and is kind of similar to the sabre engine. The reason for this thing being a hybrid is 
that due to the way the compressor is separetted from the combustion chamber, its technically both a rocket and a jet. As a result, i dont have a clear idea 
as to what i should name this as im pretty sure its the first of its kind, so imma name it something cool later on. As for the elephant in the room, this 
thing relies on hydrogen combustion thats being fed from a pem electrolizer module. alternatively, you could use an hho generator instead and seal off the 
air injector, making this a rocket. the air is coming from a 50mm 12v centrfugal blower so that it gets high speed medium pressure air ideal for hydrogen 
oxygen combustion. the air mixture for it is 2.4 liters of oxygen per 1 liter of hydrogen by volume, making it pretty easy and cheap to supply air to it. 
as for the pem module, a 100 watt module produces 1 liter of hydrogen per minute and that scales pretty linearly ( 200w = 2 liters per minute and so on...)
the combustion engine itself is made of 2 parts, the main body being petg and the end nozzle being made in polycarbonate for better heat management. since 
the whole engine is 3d printed,its water cooled to avoid turning into a sludge of plastic in like 10 seconds. the cooling system is an open loop system that 
has the water only pass one cycle before being released to avoid overheating of the plastics. overall, the parts including the water pump for the cooling 
loop cost from 160-230 euros ( 188-271$ ).
